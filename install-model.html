In-Show Integration Credibility Lift

1.30x

This adjustable assumption explores whether an in-show endorsement improves response across both QR and social channels. The lift is a planning hypothesis to validate during the pilot. This multiplier applies on top of each channel's own scan/CTR/conversion lifts below, and is already reflected in every "Modeled" figure and the combined total on this page.

At 1.30x, being woven into the actual show and its ad breaks—rather than a bare QR code or a stand-alone post—is modeled as lifting both channels' final numbers by that much versus running the same promotion with no on-air tie-in.
