01 / Audience fit

A familiar experience for an audience that already plays.

The show’s clues and guessing provide a natural entry point. A simple Daily Word, visible progress and gift-card rewards are designed around fans who enjoy casual play and a daily routine.

02 / The missed opportunity

Turn everyday play into a stronger connection to the show.

Fans already spend time playing mobile games elsewhere. Give It Away Games brings a daily reason to return to the 25 Words or Less brand—even between episodes and seasons.

Build brand loyalty

The Daily Word and streak connect a rewarding routine to the show, creating more opportunities to deepen fans’ familiarity and affinity.

Create recurring value

Eligible gameplay earns rewards for fans and generates partner revenue, giving that ongoing engagement a measurable business return.
